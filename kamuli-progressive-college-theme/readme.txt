KAMULI PROGRESSIVE COLLEGE WORDPRESS THEME
Version 1.0.0

INSTALLATION
1. In WordPress, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New > Upload Theme.
2. Upload kamuli-progressive-college-theme.zip.
3. Click Install Now, then Activate.
4. On activation, the theme creates Home, About Us, Our Services, Gallery and Contact Us pages (only if those slugs do not already exist), sets Home as the front page and creates a main navigation menu.
5. Visit Appearance > Menus (or Appearance > Editor, depending on your WordPress version) to manage navigation. You can also edit each page under Pages.

EDITING
- Edit Home, About Us, Our Services and Contact Us from Pages in the dashboard.
- Gallery images are bundled with the theme and displayed by the [kpc_gallery] shortcode on the Gallery page.
- The Contact Us page uses [kpc_contact_form]. Messages are sent to the WordPress Administration Email Address under Settings > General. Configure working website email delivery (often via an SMTP plugin) for reliable delivery.
- Replace the sample school badge via Appearance > Customize > Site Identity or the Site Logo control.

IMPORTANT
The supplied source website contained a sample phone number (+256 700 000 000) and a draft email address. These have not been published as real contact details. Add verified school contact information to the Contact Us page before launch.

This theme is designed as a standalone WordPress theme. It does not require Astra. Activating this theme replaces the active theme's visual design; your installed plugins and WordPress content remain separate.
